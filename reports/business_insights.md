# Business Recommendations

1. Protect margin on high-revenue/low-margin products by reviewing discount ceilings, bundle pricing, and supplier cost assumptions.
2. Use the Q4 demand peak to plan inventory, staffing, and campaign timing earlier in the year.
3. Create retention and cross-sell campaigns for high-value customers identified in the Pareto and RFM analyses.
4. Investigate the causes of returned and cancelled orders by product, region, and shipping mode before changing policy.
5. Coach below-target salespeople using region-adjusted benchmarks and replicate practices from consistently high-achievement peers.
6. Review discount bands above 20% because their margin profile is materially different from low-discount sales.
7. Prioritize stock availability for top products while monitoring concentration risk and substitution options.
8. Use regional profitability—not revenue alone—when allocating sales effort and promotional budgets.
9. Set delivery SLAs by shipping mode and flag Standard shipments that exceed the expected delivery range.
10. Refresh the dashboard monthly and track MoM, YoY, margin, returns, cancellations, and target achievement together rather than as isolated KPIs.
