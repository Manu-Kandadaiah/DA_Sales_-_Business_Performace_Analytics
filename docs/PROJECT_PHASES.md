# Project Phases

1. **Architecture, dataset, database, business questions** — folder structure, dimensions/measures, normalized schema, KPI questions.
2. **Synthetic data generation** — two years, weighted seasonality, customer/product/region/salesperson relationships.
3. **Python cleaning** — quality inspection, duplicates, missing values, dates, standardization, recalculation, validation.
4. **EDA & visualization** — KPI/trends/product/customer/region/salesperson/discount/order/delivery analysis and Matplotlib charts.
5. **MySQL schema & loading** — normalized tables, constraints, indexes, Python loader.
6. **SQL business analysis** — 35 executable queries with CTE/window functions.
7. **Excel dashboard** — KPI cards, summaries, formulas, professional charts and formatting.
8. **Business insights & recommendations** — only dataset-supported statements.
9. **Documentation** — README, data-quality report, metrics, presentation guidance.
10. **Interview & resume** — 30 Q&A, domain questions, SQL/Pandas/Excel questions, 5 ATS bullets.
