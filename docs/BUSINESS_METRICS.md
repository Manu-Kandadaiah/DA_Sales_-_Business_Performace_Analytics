# Business Metrics & Formulas

- **Gross Sales** = Quantity × Unit Price
- **Discount Amount** = Gross Sales × Discount %
- **Revenue / Sales Amount** = Gross Sales − Discount Amount
- **Cost** = Quantity × Unit Cost
- **Profit** = Sales Amount − Cost Amount
- **Profit Margin %** = Profit ÷ Sales Amount × 100
- **Average Order Value** = Net Revenue ÷ Number of Orders
- **Customer Lifetime Value approximation** = historical customer net revenue (portfolio proxy; not a predictive CLV model)
- **Return Rate %** = Returned Orders ÷ Total Orders × 100
- **Cancellation Rate %** = Cancelled Orders ÷ Total Orders × 100
- **Growth Rate %** = (Current Period − Previous Period) ÷ Previous Period × 100
- **Target Achievement %** = 2026 Salesperson Net Revenue ÷ 2026 Target × 100

`Net_Sales_Amount` and `Net_Profit` are additional analytical fields so cancelled and returned orders affect business reporting instead of being counted as normal sales.
