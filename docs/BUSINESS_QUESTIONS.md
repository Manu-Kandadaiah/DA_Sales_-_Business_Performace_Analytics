# Business Questions

## Revenue & Profit
- What is total revenue, profit, margin, quantity and AOV?
- How do revenue and profit vary by month, quarter and year?
## Product
- Which products/categories/brands lead revenue and profit?
- Which high-revenue products have weak margins?
## Customer
- Who are the top customers? Which segments generate revenue/profit? What does RFM show?
## Geography
- Which regions/states/cities lead, and how do margins differ?
## Salespeople
- Who exceeds target, who is below target, and how does achievement vary by region?
## Discount
- How do discount bands change revenue and margin?
## Orders & Service
- What are cancellation/return rates, payment mix, shipping mix, and delivery time?
